THE PROPOSED SOLUTION

Designed around the farmer's needs

A concept for reducing avoidable post-harvest losses where reliable cold-storage access is limited.

☀️

Solar-assisted power

Proposed solar panel, charge controller and battery to support cooling when grid power is unreliable.

🌡️

Smart monitoring

Planned temperature, humidity, door and battery monitoring using sensors and an ESP32 controller.

🥬

Temporary produce storage

A target capacity of 30–50 kg for vegetables before transport or sale, subject to engineering validation.

📊

Decision support

Future software could show alerts and support store-versus-sell decisions using crop and market information.
